// About

Behind the timeline for years — not just cut and paste.

I'm Fathalla, a video editor based in Egypt. It started back in 2022 as a hobby — cutting random clips just to see how far I could push a story with nothing but timing and music. Somewhere along the way it turned into a craft, then a career.

Since then I've been behind the timeline for brands, creators, and small businesses — chasing the same feeling every time: that split-second where a cut just hits right. Every project is different, but the goal never changes — making the viewer feel something, frame by frame.
